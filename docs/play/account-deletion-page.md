# Delete your Doomscroll Duel account and data

This page is for **Doomscroll Duel**, an app by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}. You can delete your account and all the data we hold about you. You do not need to be signed in to read this page.

## Fastest way: inside the App (takes about a minute)

1. Open **Doomscroll Duel**.
2. Go to **Settings**, then **Account delete karo**.
3. Read the list of what will be deleted, type **DELETE**, tick the box and tap **ACCOUNT DELETE KARO**.
4. If asked, sign in again (this is a security check so nobody else can delete your account).

Deletion runs straight away. When it finishes, you see "Account delete ho gaya".

## If you cannot open the App (lost phone, uninstalled)

Email **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}** from the email address or phone-linked account you used to sign up, with the subject **"Delete my account"**. Please include your username. We will ask you to confirm it is you (for example by replying from the same email or telling us details only the account owner knows). We reply within **7 days** and finish deletion within **30 days** of confirming it is you.

## What is deleted

- Your profile, display name, username and avatar
- Your friends list and invites
- Your duel, squad and pact history and the reel counts we stored for them
- Your coin balance and unlock requests
- Your Pro record and Google Play purchase token
- Push notification tokens
- Dare proof photos and videos (these are anyway deleted automatically after 7 days)
- Your sign-in account itself

## What is not deleted, and why

- **Your Google Play subscription.** Only Google can cancel it. Please also cancel in Google Play > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions, or you may be charged again.
- **Other players' own copy of a shared result.** In their history your name becomes "deleted"; they keep their own record of their own game.
- **Data on your phone** (settings, local reel history). Uninstalling the App removes it.
- **Short technical logs.** Our function logs keep only a one-way hash of your account ID for up to 30 days, then they are removed.

We do not keep any other copy of your personal data, and we do not keep separate backups.
