## 4. The Accessibility service (important)

To count reels and to block them when you ask, the App uses Android's Accessibility service. Before you are asked to turn it on, the App shows a full-screen explanation and you must tap **Agree**. If you tap **Not now**, the counter stays off and nothing is requested.

What the service does:
- Counts how many reels or short videos you swipe to in **Instagram, YouTube (Shorts), Facebook and Snapchat**. Android delivers events to us only from these four apps.
- Only if you turn on Strict Lock, Bedtime mode, Focus hours or the Wait-10 gate: shows a "Brain bachao" screen and presses **Back** or **Home** when a reel screen opens in one of those four apps.

What the service sees: which of the four apps is open, that a scroll happened, the technical name of the scrolled view, and whether a reels screen is on screen. 

What it does not do: it does not read text, messages, passwords, names, captions, comments or what you watch; it does not record or screenshot the screen; it does not tap, type or change anything for you; it does not look at any other app.

The only thing that leaves your phone is the reel count described in section 3. You can switch the service off at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility.

## 7. Virtual coins and Pro

Coins are virtual points used inside the App. They have no cash value, cannot be bought with money, cannot be sold or cashed out, and are not given by a Pro subscription. The App does not involve betting with money. Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play. It renews automatically until you cancel it in Google Play. Deleting your App account does not cancel a Google Play subscription, so please cancel it in Google Play as well.

## 9. How to delete your account and data

1. Open the App, go to **Settings**, then **Account delete karo**.
2. Read what will be deleted, type **DELETE** and confirm. For security you may need to sign in again first.
3. We delete your profile, username, friends list, duel history, server reel counts, coins, Pro record, purchase token, push tokens and dare proofs, then your sign-in account.

If you cannot open the App, follow the steps at {{SITE_URL}}/delete-account, or email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the address linked to your account. We reply within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days.

**What deletion does not do:** it does not cancel a Google Play subscription (cancel it in Google Play); it does not remove other players' own copy of a shared result (your name in it becomes "deleted"); it keeps no copy of your personal data on our servers.

## 10. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to our database is controlled by security rules, and only our server functions can change coins and Pro status. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is less to lose. If a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
