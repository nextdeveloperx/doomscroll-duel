# Play Console: store listing text

Copy each block exactly. Character limits are Google's: app name 30, short description 80, full description 4000.
`scripts/check-play-readiness.sh` counts the lengths and refuses claims it knows get apps rejected (medical, gambling and
"guaranteed" wording). Everything between the START/END markers is scanned.

Keep the listing honest: screenshots must show the real app, no fake counters, no "#1", no testimonials, no rewards for
installing, no price in the title. The name "Doomscroll Duel" is a working name: before launch check that it is free to
use and does not copy someone else's trademark.

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE DISCLOSURE

## Graphic and screenshot rules
- Screenshots: real screens only (Home, Battle modes, New duel, Live duel, Result, Settings, Paywall, the disclosure). Add
  captions, not claims. Do not show other apps' logos large; do not mimic their UI.
- Feature graphic: the brain mascot and the app name. No "#1", "best", "free coins", no price, no medical imagery.
- Do not use "download now", countdowns, or text about rewards.

## Words to avoid anywhere in the listing, screenshots and in-app copy
Medical or therapy: addiction, cure, treat, treatment, therapy, detox, depression, anxiety, ADHD, mental health, clinical,
diagnose (the only use allowed is the sentence "does not diagnose, treat or cure anything").
Gambling: bet, betting, wager, stake, jackpot, odds, casino, winnings (use "entry coins" and "virtual coins"; saying coins cannot be cashed out is fine).
Absolute claims: guaranteed, proven, scientifically, doctor, "will make you focus", "quit reels for ever".
